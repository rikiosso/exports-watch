🇺🇸 BIS removes two Hong Kong addresses from Arrow Electronics (Hong Kong) Co., Ltd. Entity List entry (China)

📅 24 August 2026 · added to archive 22 August 2026 · high confidence

What changed:

  • BIS revised the Entity List entry for Arrow Electronics (Hong Kong) Co., Ltd. under the destination of China by removing two addresses — 2/F and 3/F, Green 18, Phase 2, Hong Kong Science Park, Hong Kong; and Units 11001-11008E & 11001-11012W, 11th Floor, ATL Logistics Centre B, No. 8 Container Port Road South, Kwai Chung, Hong Kong — following ERC review under Sec. 744.16 of the EAR [FR Doc. 2026-17231, Removals From the Entity List].
  • This follows the November 2025 removal of Arrow China Electronics Trading Co., Ltd. and six aliases for Arrow Electronics (Hong Kong) Co., Ltd. [FR Doc. 2026-17231, Background].

Who is affected:

  • Persons exporting, reexporting, or transferring (in-country) items subject to the EAR to Arrow Electronics (Hong Kong) Co., Ltd., which remains on the Entity List under China at its five other listed addresses with a license requirement for all items subject to the EAR and a presumption-of-denial review policy [FR Doc. 2026-17231, Supplement No. 4 to Part 744].
  • Parties transacting solely at the two removed addresses are no longer subject to the Entity List licensing requirement tied to this entry [FR Doc. 2026-17231, Removals From the Entity List].

Effective: August 21, 2026

Instruments: Entity List (Supplement No. 4 to 15 CFR Part 744)

Relevance for EU exporters: The Entity List imposes license requirements on exports, reexports, and transfers (in-country) of items subject to the EAR when a listed entity is a party to the transaction; this applies regardless of the exporter's location [FR Doc. 2026-17231, Background]. EU exporters reexporting or transferring items subject to the EAR to Arrow Electronics (Hong Kong) Co., Ltd. at its remaining listed addresses remain subject to this license requirement; transactions solely involving the two removed addresses no longer trigger it [FR Doc. 2026-17231, Removals From the Entity List].

Δ Arrow Electronics (Hong Kong) Co., Ltd. — China entry: “Listed addresses included: 20/F, Tower 2, Evergreen Plaza, 88 Container Port Road, Kwai Chung, Hong Kong; Lot 204, DD105, Castle Peak Road, Yuen Long, Hong Kong; Unit 1003, Kerry Cargo Center, 55 Wing Kei Road, Kwai Chung, Hong Kong; Unit 5001-5004W, 5th Floor, ATL Logistics Centre A, No. 8 Container Port Road South, Kwai Chung, Hong Kong; Unit 5015E-5020E, 5th Floor, ATL Logistics Centre B, No. 8 Container Port Road South, Kwai Chung, Hong Kong; 2/F and 3/F, Green 18, Phase 2, Hong Kong Science Park, Hong Kong; Units 11001-11008E & 11001-11012W, 11th Floor, ATL Logistics Centre B, No. 8 Container Port Road South, Kwai Chung, Hong Kong” → “Listed addresses limited to: 20/F, Tower 2, Evergreen Plaza, 88 Container Port Road, Kwai Chung, Hong Kong; Lot 204, DD105, Castle Peak Road, Yuen Long, Hong Kong; Unit 1003, Kerry Cargo Center, 55 Wing Kei Road, Kwai Chung, Hong Kong; Unit 5001-5004W, 5th Floor, ATL Logistics Centre A, No. 8 Container Port Road South, Kwai Chung, Hong Kong; Unit 5015E-5020E, 5th Floor, ATL Logistics Centre B, No. 8 Container Port Road South, Kwai Chung, Hong Kong”

Action items:

  • Cross-check counterparty addresses against the two removed addresses (Hong Kong Science Park; ATL Logistics Centre B units 11001-11008E/11001-11012W) before concluding Entity List exposure has ended.
  • Confirm the five remaining listed addresses for Arrow Electronics (Hong Kong) Co., Ltd. before proceeding with any export, reexport, or in-country transfer of items subject to the EAR.
  • Maintain presumption-of-denial screening for transactions involving Arrow Electronics (Hong Kong) Co., Ltd. at its remaining listed addresses.
