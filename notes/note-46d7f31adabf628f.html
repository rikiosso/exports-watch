🇺🇸 US cuts UK-origin patented pharmaceutical tariff from 10% to 0% under Proclamation 11020

📅 04 August 2026 · high confidence

What changed: The notice revises the tariff rate on patented pharmaceuticals and associated pharmaceutical ingredients that are products of the United Kingdom from 10 percent to zero percent, amending Harmonized Tariff Schedule heading 9903.04.63 by deleting "+10%" and inserting "+0%" [FR Doc. 2026-15799, Modifications to the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States].

Who is affected: Importers of patented pharmaceuticals and associated pharmaceutical ingredients that are products of the United Kingdom entering the United States for consumption or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption on or after the effective date [FR Doc. 2026-15799, DATES].

Effective: 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on July 31, 2026

Instruments: Presidential Proclamation 11020 "Adjusting Imports of Pharmaceuticals and Pharmaceutical Ingredients Into the United States"; Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States, heading 9903.04.63

Relevance for EU exporters: indirect only
