🇺🇸 United States · Rule change · 17 September 2026

BIS renews Azur Air temporary denial order for one year (Russia aviation, ECCN 9A991)

What changed

BIS granted the Office of Export Enforcement's request to renew the temporary denial order issued on September 16, 2025 against Azur Air [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Preamble].

Effective

Order dated September 11, 2026; "This Order is effective immediately and shall remain in effect for one year." (published September 17, 2026)

Who is affected

The named respondent is Azur Air, Office 29, Vzletnaya St. 57, Krasnoyarsk, Russia 660020 [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section IV].

What changed

  • BIS granted the Office of Export Enforcement's request to renew the temporary denial order issued on September 16, 2025 against Azur Air [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Preamble].
  • The renewal runs for one year [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section IV].
  • OEE submitted its written renewal request on August 3, 2026 [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section I].
  • The order states that, given the temporary suspension of international mail service to Russia, OEE attempted to deliver the request to Azur by alternative means, and that no opposition to the renewal was received [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section I].
  • The order records the TDO history as an initial 180-day order signed April 7, 2022, renewed on October 3, 2022, March 29, 2023, September 23, 2023, September 20, 2024 and September 16, 2025 [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section I].
  • The order states that this renewal reflects a corrected address for Azur, which was introduced by a modification of the September 20, 2024 renewal order dated January 31, 2025 in the text (footnote 5 refers to a January 30, 2025 modification order) [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section I].
  • The Assistant Secretary found that BIS's evidence convincingly demonstrates that Azur violated the Regulations and the TDO, that the violations were significant and deliberate, and that there is a likelihood of imminent violations [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section III].
  • The order bases the one-year period on a finding that Azur engaged in a pattern of repeated, ongoing and/or continuous apparent violations of the EAR [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section III].
  • The order's table lists flights dated March 9, 2026 to July 17, 2026 by Boeing 757 aircraft (tail numbers RA-73071, RA-73029, RA-73076 and RA-73072) between Russia and Turkey, Vietnam or Thailand, and within Russia [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section II.B].
  • The order is dated September 11, 2026 and is effective immediately [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section IV].

Who is affected

  • The named respondent is Azur Air, Office 29, Vzletnaya St. 57, Krasnoyarsk, Russia 660020 [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section IV].
  • Under the order's first paragraph, Azur, when acting for or on its behalf, and any successors or assigns, agents or employees may not directly or indirectly participate in any transaction involving items subject to the EAR that are exported or to be exported from the United States, or in any other activity subject to the EAR [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section IV, First].
  • Under the second paragraph, no person may export, reexport or transfer (in-country) any item subject to the EAR to or on behalf of Azur, except as directly related to safety of flight and authorized by BIS under Section 764.3(a)(2) [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section IV, Second.A].
  • The same paragraph also restricts any person from facilitating Azur's acquisition of, acquiring from Azur, or servicing items subject to the EAR that have been or will be exported from the United States, subject to the same safety-of-flight exception [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section IV, Second.B-E].
  • Any other person related to Azur by ownership, control, position of responsibility, affiliation or other connection in the conduct of trade or business may be made subject to the order after notice and opportunity for comment under Section 766.23 [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section IV, Third].
  • The tail numbers and serial numbers listed in the table are RA-73071 (SN 29377), RA-73029 (SN 26330), RA-73076 (SN 30043) and RA-73072 (SN 29382) [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section II.B].

Instruments

Order Renewing Temporary Denial of Export Privileges (15 CFR 766.24); Export Administration Regulations, 15 CFR parts 730-774; Export Control Reform Act of 2018, 50 U.S.C. 4820(a)(5); 15 CFR 746.8 (Russia aircraft and aircraft parts, ECCN 9A991); 15 CFR 740.15 (license exception AVS); 15 CFR 764.3(a)(2) (safety-of-flight authorization)

Relevance for EU exporters

The second paragraph of the order applies to "no person" with respect to items subject to the EAR that have been or will be exported from the United States, and the order states that it gives notice to companies and individuals in the United States and abroad to avoid dealing with Azur [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Sections III and IV]. The only content-based mechanism cited is the 25% controlled U.S.-origin content threshold for foreign aircraft travelling to Russia, and no foreign direct product rule or de minimis provision is invoked in relation to the order itself [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section II.B]. Otherwise: indirect only.

Action items

  • Screen counterparties, and their owners, affiliates and agents, against Azur Air and its listed address before any transaction involving items subject to the EAR [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section IV].
  • Treat any service, financing, acquisition or export involving Azur and items subject to the EAR as within the scope of the order, unless directly related to safety of flight and authorized by BIS under Section 764.3(a)(2) [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section IV, Second.A-E].
  • Note that BIS may seek further renewal by written request not later than 20 days before expiration, and that Azur may oppose by a submission received not later than seven days before expiration [FR Doc. 2026-19021, Section IV].
