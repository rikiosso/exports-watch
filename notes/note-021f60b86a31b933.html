🇪🇺 European Union · Annex I adoption · 14 September 2026

Commission adopts 2026 update of EU dual-use control list (Annex I, Reg. 2021/821); not yet in force

What changed

On 14 September 2026 the European Commission adopted a Delegated Regulation updating the EU dual-use export control list in Annex I of Regulation (EU) 2021/821 [C(2026)6323].

Adopted (not yet in force)

Adopted by the Commission: 14 September 2026 (adoption date, not an in-force date) [C(2026)6323]; Entry into force: "upon publication in the Official Journal after the usual two-month scrutiny period for the Council and the European Parliament"; no calendar date stated in source [C(2026)6323]

Who is affected

The announcement states that the EU control list is a common list of dual-use items that must be controlled in all Member States [C(2026)6323].

What changed

  • On 14 September 2026 the European Commission adopted a Delegated Regulation updating the EU dual-use export control list in Annex I of Regulation (EU) 2021/821 [C(2026)6323].
  • The announcement states that the update aligns the list with 2025 decisions of the Wassenaar Arrangement, the Australia Group and the Nuclear Suppliers Group, and includes commitments Member States accepted as Wassenaar members to control additional items uniformly [C(2026)6323].
  • The announcement states that the update adds new dual-use items, including semiconductor manufacturing and testing equipment and materials, advanced computing integrated circuits and electronic assemblies such as ICs incorporating one or multiple Digital Processing Units, ceramic matrix composites reinforced with mullite, inductive-sensing rotary encoders, additive manufacturing equipment for energetic materials, chemical vapour deposition equipment for silicon carbide fibres, and technology for the development of axial compressors of gas turbine engines [C(2026)6323].
  • The semiconductor examples given are atomic layer deposition equipment for molybdenum and ruthenium, equipment for the development and inspection of Extreme Ultra-Violet masks and reticles, and single wafer cleaning equipment [C(2026)6323].
  • The announcement also states that certain control parameters are modified and certain technical definitions and descriptions are updated [C(2026)6323].
  • The act was adopted by the Commission and is not yet published in the Official Journal or in force; it enters into force upon publication in the Official Journal after the usual two-month scrutiny period for the Council and the European Parliament [C(2026)6323].

Who is affected

  • The announcement states that the EU control list is a common list of dual-use items that must be controlled in all Member States [C(2026)6323].
  • It refers to uniform EU-level controls and a level-playing-field for economic operators, without naming specific operators, entry codes or destinations [C(2026)6323].

Licence posture

The announcement states no licence requirement, licence exception, presumption or case-by-case policy [C(2026)6323]. It states only that the listed items are added to the EU control list, which must be controlled in all Member States [C(2026)6323].

Relevance for EU exporters

The act amends Annex I of Regulation (EU) 2021/821, the common EU control list, and applies through that list once it enters into force after publication in the Official Journal [C(2026)6323]. The announcement does not state any foreign direct product rule, de minimis, transshipment or remote-access provision [C(2026)6323]. Entry-level text, thresholds and the Comprehensive Change Note Summary are not reproduced in the source provided and are not stated in source [C(2026)6323].
