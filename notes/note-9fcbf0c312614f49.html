🇺🇸 United States · Rule change · 23 July 2026

BIS moves firearm sound suppressors from ITAR to EAR, adds new ECCNs and TMP tools-of-trade exception

What changed

BIS revised the EAR and CCL to control certain silencers, mufflers, and sound suppressors that are being removed from the USML by a companion State Department IFR, creating new item paragraphs 0A501.f (suppressors usable with rifles/pistols under 0A501, 0A506, 0A507, or interchangeably with shotguns) and 0A502.f (suppressors for exclusive use with shotguns under 0A502/0A508) [FR Doc. 2026-14942, SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION, Section I.B].

Effective

November 20, 2026 (general effective date, including new ECCN 0A501.f/0A502.f item paragraphs and Sec. 742.6(a)(14)/(b)(15)); July 23, 2026 (amendatory instructions 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, and 11, including Sec. 740.9 TMP revision, Sec. 740.14 BAG revision, Sec. 758.1 and 758.10 revisions, and the July 23, 2026 version of ECCN 0A502); Comments due August 24, 2026; Savings clause deadline: August 24, 2026 for shipments en route as of July 23, 2026 under prior NLR/license exception eligibility

Who is affected

Exporters, reexporters, and manufacturers of firearm sound suppressors and related firearms parts/components/accessories/attachments classified under ECCNs 0A501, 0A502, 0A506, 0A507, and 0A508; employers whose employees carry company-owned firearms abroad as tools of trade; and temporary importers of firearms-related items that are also listed on the USMIL [FR Doc. 2026-14942, SUMMARY; Sections I-III].

What changed

  • BIS revised the EAR and CCL to control certain silencers, mufflers, and sound suppressors that are being removed from the USML by a companion State Department IFR, creating new item paragraphs 0A501.f (suppressors usable with rifles/pistols under 0A501, 0A506, 0A507, or interchangeably with shotguns) and 0A502.f (suppressors for exclusive use with shotguns under 0A502/0A508) [FR Doc. 2026-14942, SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION, Section I.B].
  • New reasons for control (NS1/RS1/FC1/UN/AT1 for 0A501.f; FC1/UN/AT1 plus a new RS control for 0A502.f) and license exception eligibility (LVS, BAG, STA, TMP) were added for the new paragraphs [FR Doc. 2026-14942, Section I.C-D].
  • BIS also revised License Exception TMP (Sec. 740.9) to add a new paragraph (a)(2) authorizing temporary export/reexport of firearms and related items, including suppressors, as 'tools of trade' without a license, subject to quantity limits and effective-control/return conditions [FR Doc. 2026-14942, Section II].
  • Sec. 758.10 entry-clearance requirements for temporary imports were broadened from listed end-item ECCNs to all 0x5zz and certain '600 series' items subject to the EAR that are also on the USMIL [FR Doc. 2026-14942, Section III].

Who is affected

Exporters, reexporters, and manufacturers of firearm sound suppressors and related firearms parts/components/accessories/attachments classified under ECCNs 0A501, 0A502, 0A506, 0A507, and 0A508; employers whose employees carry company-owned firearms abroad as tools of trade; and temporary importers of firearms-related items that are also listed on the USMIL [FR Doc. 2026-14942, SUMMARY; Sections I-III].

Instruments

15 CFR 740 (License Exceptions, including Sec. 740.9 TMP and Sec. 740.14 BAG); 15 CFR 742 (Sec. 742.6 Regional Stability, Sec. 742.7 Crime Control); 15 CFR 758 (Sec. 758.1 EEI/AES filing, Sec. 758.10 entry clearance for temporary imports); 15 CFR 774 Supplement No. 1 (CCL: ECCNs 0A501, 0A502, 0A506, 0A507, 0A508)

Relevance for EU exporters

The new RS control at Sec. 742.6(a)(14) exempts exports/reexports of ECCN 0A502.f sound suppressors from the license requirement otherwise applicable to non-listed destinations for NATO member states, Australia, Austria, India, Ireland, Japan, Liechtenstein, New Zealand, South Korea, or Switzerland, with NATO membership determined by cross-reference to Sec. 772.1 of the EAR [FR Doc. 2026-14942, Section I.E; amendment 5, Sec. 742.6(a)(14)]. The source does not identify which countries are NATO members, does not discuss EU membership, and does not state that EU member states are covered by this exemption; any link to EU exporters via this NATO carve-out is therefore indirect only. Beyond this, the source does not address other EU-specific mechanisms such as a foreign direct product rule or de minimis threshold for these ECCNs.

Action items

  • Reclassify any silencers/mufflers/sound suppressors previously handled under State/ITAR jurisdiction to determine whether they now fall under ECCN 0A501.f or 0A502.f.
  • Confirm, via Sec. 772.1 of the EAR, whether the destination country qualifies as a NATO member state (or is otherwise listed in Sec. 742.6(a)(14)) before relying on the exemption from the RS license requirement for 0A502.f items, while confirming other reasons for control (NS, FC1, UN, AT1) still apply.
  • Review use of License Exceptions LVS, BAG, STA, and revised TMP (Sec. 740.9(a)(2)) for compliance with quantity limits, effective-control, and return-to-US/reexport-country conditions before relying on them for suppressor or firearms shipments.
  • For temporary imports of 0x5zz or '600 series' items also on the USMIL, apply the broadened Sec. 758.10 entry clearance requirements rather than seeking a separate ATF permit.
  • Monitor the comment period (due August 24, 2026) and the savings clause deadline (August 24, 2026) for shipments en route as of July 23, 2026.
  • Anticipate increased licensing volume: BIS projects an increase of approximately 200 license applications annually from the sound-suppressor additions, offset by an expected decrease of approximately 40 license applications annually from the new TMP tools-of-trade exception.
