🇺🇸 BIS opens comment period on information collection for DPAS critical-minerals allocation orders

📅 25 August 2026 · medium confidence

What changed:

  • The Department of Commerce/Bureau of Industry and Security published a notice allowing 60 days of public comment on the OMB-approved emergency information collection associated with the Temporary Final Rule 'DPAS Directive Allocation Order and Additional Requirements for Recoverable Critical Minerals and Materials' (August 6, 2026, 91 FR 50701) [FR Doc. 2026-17323, Summary].
  • The collection implements recordkeeping and submission requirements under section 700.91 of the DPAS regulation tied to Presidential Determination No. 2026-19 on recoverable Critical Minerals and Materials [FR Doc. 2026-17323, I. Abstract].

Who is affected: Businesses or other for-profit organizations subject to Directive Allocation Orders issued under the Defense Priorities and Allocations System regulation, with an estimated 600 respondents [FR Doc. 2026-17323, III. Data].

Effective: Comments must be received on or before October 26, 2026

Instruments: DPAS Directive Allocation Orders; Temporary Final Rule (91 FR 50701, August 6, 2026); Presidential Determination No. 2026-19; Title I of the Defense Production Act of 1950, as amended

Relevance for EU exporters: indirect only

Action items:

  • Submit comments on the information collection to Nancy Kook, IC Liaison, BIS, by October 26, 2026, referencing OMB Control Number 0694-0148 [FR Doc. 2026-17323, ADDRESSES]
  • Review recordkeeping obligations under section 700.91 of the DPAS regulation as detailed in the August 6, 2026 Temporary Final Rule [FR Doc. 2026-17323, I. Abstract]
